From depth to time

Two ways of turning a video into a spatial object in Blender: first as a depth relief that plays in real time, then as a volume of time that can be cut, tilted and swept.

Environment
Blender 5.2, Geometry Nodes
Footage
SDR video, 2 to 20 s
Methods
A depth point cloud, B time-slice volume
Stage
Exploration, phase 1

Fig. 1 Process and scope

The scope moved from making a video play inside space to making time itself a material.

Fig. 2 Exploration A: depth point cloud

A plane is scattered with points. A depth video lifts each point along Z, a colour video paints it, and both movie textures read the scene frame, so the cloud plays with the timeline.

Fig. 3 Method study B: time as an axis

Every frame is a flat X·Y plane; the frames stack along Z. What the slice shows depends on where it cuts and at what angle.
